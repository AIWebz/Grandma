What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you choose, if you create an account.
  • What you put in the app: conversations with Grandma, tasks, grocery lists, planner items, saved recipes, Family Cookbook entries and photos, and the preferences Grandma remembers (like foods you dislike).
  • Subscription status: your plan and renewal date. Payment details are handled by Apple, Google, or Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  • Usage counts: how many AI messages you've sent today, to apply plan limits.
  • Advertising data (Free plan): ad partners may collect device identifiers and ad interactions, subject to your consent choices.

We ask Grandma not to remember sensitive information such as health conditions (beyond food allergies or diets you mention), finances, passwords, or ID numbers.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: generate Grandma's replies, keep your lists and recipes, and sync them across your devices and household.
  • To apply your plan's features and limits, and to show or remove ads.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you turn on.

We don't sell your personal information.
